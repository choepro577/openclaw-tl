# Official Sources

Use this file when you need more detail on where to look and how to search.

## 1. `vbpl.vn`

Use this as the default primary source for Vietnamese legal documents.

What it is good for:

- Full text of laws and sub-law documents
- Document metadata
- Effective status
- Related-document chains such as amendment, supplementation, replacement, and annulment
- Central and local normative legal documents
- Consolidated texts when available

What to extract:

- Exact title
- Number or symbol
- Document type
- Issuing body
- Signing date
- Effective date
- Status text shown by the official page
- Related documents that change the status or content

Preferred use:

- Start here when the user names a specific document.
- Start here after topic discovery on another official source.
- Use it to confirm the legally operative source after finding a recent announcement elsewhere.

## 2. `phapdien.moj.gov.vn`

Use this for subject-matter research when the user asks by topic instead of by document number.

What it is good for:

- Finding currently effective codified provisions grouped by topic
- Building a quick map of controlling rules in a practice area
- Reducing the risk of missing a key operative provision when the user asks broad questions

How to use it:

- Search the legal topic or concept.
- Identify the relevant codified provision.
- Trace that provision back to the originating law, decree, circular, or other source document.
- Confirm the source document and status on `vbpl.vn`.

Do not stop at the codified page if the user needs citation-ready legal research. Map back to the source document.

## 3. `congbao.chinhphu.vn`

Use this when publication matters or when you need to confirm that a document appeared in the Official Gazette.

What it is good for:

- Official gazette issue number and issue date
- Publication trail for newly issued documents
- Cross-checking whether a very recent document has been formally published

Use this especially when:

- The user asks how new a document is
- The timing of publication matters
- You need a second official confirmation for a newly issued document

## 4. `xaydungchinhsach.chinhphu.vn`

Use this as a recent-document discovery layer, not as the final word on legal status.

What it is good for:

- Official "toan van" posts
- Official summaries of newly passed or newly issued laws and decrees
- Recent policy and legislative updates

Workflow:

- Find the official post or announcement.
- Capture the exact title, number, and date if shown.
- Verify the binding text and current status on `vbpl.vn` or another primary official source.

## 5. Other official portals

Use official ministry, National Assembly, Government, court, or provincial portals when the primary databases lag or when you need a supplemental official document trail.

Examples of helpful supplemental official portals:

- Government portal pages for decrees and decisions
- National Assembly pages for adopted laws and resolutions
- Ministry portals for circulars, guidance, and implementation materials
- Provincial portals for local normative documents

When using a supplemental portal, say why it was needed and still check `vbpl.vn` when possible.

For economic-law and F&B work, supplemental official portals often matter when you need:

- business registration or enterprise-operation guidance
- tax, invoicing, customs, or import administration materials
- food-safety or product-compliance guidance
- alcohol, tobacco, labeling, promotion, or advertising implementation details
- provincial licensing, inspection, or enforcement notices

## Search Patterns

Use quoted queries and domain filters when exact matching matters.

Patterns:

- `site:vbpl.vn "so ky hieu"`
- `site:vbpl.vn "ten van ban" "nam"`
- `site:vbpl.vn "co hieu luc"`
- `site:phapdien.moj.gov.vn "chu de phap ly"`
- `site:congbao.chinhphu.vn "so ky hieu"`
- `site:xaydungchinhsach.chinhphu.vn "toan van" "ten van ban"`

If the result set is noisy:

- Add the issuing body
- Add the document type
- Add the year
- Search the full Vietnamese title in quotes

## Recency Rules

Apply these rules to every current-law question:

- Treat every "latest", "current", or "still effective" question as web-verification required.
- Prefer source dates over your own relative interpretation.
- Quote exact dates from the source page.
- If a source shows a date that appears ahead of your local time, report the source date as written instead of normalizing it informally.

## Status Vocabulary

Use the official status wording where possible, for example:

- `con hieu luc`
- `het hieu luc toan bo`
- `het hieu luc mot phan`
- `bi thay the`
- `bi sua doi, bo sung`

If an official page does not explicitly state status, describe only the verified facts and avoid inventing a status label.
