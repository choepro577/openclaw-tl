# Economic Law And F&B Focus

Use this file when the request is about a Vietnamese business operator, especially an F&B business.

## Triage First

Before searching, classify the task across these axes:

- Operator type: restaurant, cafe, cloud kitchen, manufacturer, processor, importer, distributor, wholesaler, retailer, franchise system, or e-commerce seller
- Product type: ordinary food, beverage, alcohol, beer, tobacco-adjacent sales, packaged goods, ingredients, or mixed menu items
- Channel: dine-in, takeaway, delivery app, wholesale, retail shelf, distributor, online store, or franchise network
- Geography: nationwide rule only, or location-specific licensing and inspections
- Workstream: setup, license, product compliance, labeling, promotion, contract, labor, tax, dispute, or enforcement response

If the user does not specify these details, infer the narrowest reasonable operator model and say that you are assuming it.

## Legal Stack

Build the research stack in this order:

1. Horizontal economic-law layer
   Enterprise, investment, commerce, contracts, tax, invoicing, labor, consumer protection, competition, e-commerce, and advertising
2. F&B sector layer
   Food safety, product standards or declarations where applicable, labeling, product quality, alcohol, tobacco, traceability, import or customs, and inspection rules
3. Operating-premise layer
   Local licensing, food-safety conditions, fire safety, environment, hygiene, signage, and other site-specific controls
4. Transaction layer
   Promotions, sales claims, distributor terms, franchise structure, platform terms, returns, complaints, and enforcement exposure

## Typical F&B Question Types

Treat these as recurring patterns:

- Can this restaurant or cafe operate with its current licenses?
- Does this product need a declaration, registration, label change, or testing file?
- Can the business advertise or promote this product the way it wants?
- Is a liquor-related activity sale, distribution, storage, import, or promotion allowed under current rules?
- Which rules apply differently to a manufacturer, importer, distributor, and retail outlet?
- What documents control consumer complaints, recalls, quality disputes, or administrative inspections?

## What To Pull From Sources

For economic-law and F&B answers, extract not only the legal citation but also the operational consequence:

- Who is regulated
- What activity triggers the rule
- Whether a permit, registration, notification, training, record, label, or internal process is required
- The effective date and current status
- Whether the rule is central or local
- What the next compliance step appears to be

## Output Priorities For Legal Agents

After the legal summary, add a short practical block covering:

- `Tac dong van hanh`
- `Giay phep/ho so can kiem tra them`
- `Rui ro neu lam sai`

Keep this practical block short and source-grounded. Do not invent procedures that the official sources do not support.
