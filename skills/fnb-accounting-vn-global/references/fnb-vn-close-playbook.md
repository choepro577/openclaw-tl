## 1. Close cadence

- Run a `daily flash` view for sales, collections, voids, discounts, refunds, and key stock movements.
- Run a `weekly control` view for inventory shrinkage, merchant settlement delays, unpaid supplier balances, and unusual journals.
- Run a `hard close` at month end with a documented cutoff date for invoices, receipts, bank statements, payroll, and store confirmations.

## 2. Daily controls

- Lock the POS day by store and channel before posting revenue summaries.
- Reconcile daily sales to:
  - POS or OMS order count
  - Cash count and safe drop
  - Card or e-wallet settlement file
  - Delivery platform daily statement
- Review abnormal voids, refunds, negative checks, free-of-charge items, and manual price overrides.
- Confirm inventory issues or transfers posted by stores or the central kitchen on the correct date.

## 3. Weekly controls

- Compare actual food cost percent against recipe or theoretical food cost percent by store and major category.
- Review stock adjustment trends, expired goods, spoilage, and inter-store transfers.
- Check unbilled receipts, goods received not invoiced, and supplier statements for missing AP.
- Review bank settlement aging for cards and delivery platforms.
- Review payroll anomalies:
  - headcount spikes
  - unusual overtime
  - service charge pool movement
  - staff meals and related benefits

## 4. Month-end checklist

- Revenue:
  - Reconcile monthly sales by store, channel, and VAT rate.
  - Match delivery-platform statements to gross sales, commissions, vouchers, and cash remitted.
  - Verify gift-card or stored-value liabilities, breakage policy, and redemption movement.
- Cash and bank:
  - Complete bank reconciliation for every account.
  - Clear stale reconciling items and identify unresolved merchant-settlement delays.
  - Confirm petty cash counts and approvals.
- Inventory:
  - Freeze stock transactions for counted locations.
  - Capture physical count adjustments with reason codes.
  - Reconcile warehouse, central kitchen, and store balances.
- AP and expenses:
  - Accrue utilities, rent, shared services, commissions, and unbilled goods or services.
  - Separate prepaids, deposits, capex, and opex.
- Payroll:
  - Reconcile payroll register to GL and bank payments.
  - Confirm service charge or tip distribution and related liabilities.
- Tax support:
  - Verify VAT invoice completeness, canceled invoices, replacement invoices, and rate mapping.
  - Prepare supporting schedules for VAT, withholding, PIT, CIT, and other local declarations as applicable.

## 5. Revenue and settlement reconciliation

- Start from transaction volume:
  - guest checks
  - orders
  - channel mix
  - average check
- Then bridge to money movement:
  - cash in hand
  - bank settlement
  - platform receivable
  - card receivable
  - gift-card liability
- Reconcile gross-to-net revenue using:
  - promotional discounts
  - coupons
  - complimentary items
  - loyalty redemptions
  - delivery or marketplace commissions
  - refunds and chargebacks
- Separate timing differences from genuine leakage:
  - settlement next month
  - invoice issued later
  - order recorded in one day and delivered in another

## 6. Inventory and COGS reconciliation

- Build the bridge:
  `opening stock + purchases + transfers in - transfers out - ending stock = usage at cost`
- Compare usage at cost to sales mix and recipe mix.
- Inspect these F&B-specific drivers:
  - recipe change without cost update
  - yield assumptions too optimistic
  - unit conversion errors
  - central-kitchen transfer pricing
  - wastage and spoilage
  - expired goods
  - staff consumption
  - promotional bundles not reflected in recipes
- Escalate immediately when you see:
  - negative inventory
  - repeated manual stock corrections
  - margin drop without mix change
  - same item with multiple inconsistent UOMs

## 7. AP, expenses, and accruals

- Tie supplier invoices to receiving evidence wherever possible.
- For non-PO environments, build supplier reconciliation from:
  - invoice list
  - receiving log
  - supplier statement
  - bank payment detail
- Accrue recurring expenses even if invoices arrive late:
  - rent
  - CAM or service charges
  - electricity, water, gas, internet
  - cloud software or POS fees
  - delivery commissions
  - corporate allocations
- Review manual journals for round-number accruals lacking support.

## 8. Payroll and statutory support

- Reconcile payroll master to HR roster and attendance or scheduling data.
- Separate permanent staff, casual staff, outsourced labor, and management payroll.
- Track service charge, tip pool, meal allowance, uniform deductions, and one-off settlements distinctly.
- Maintain support packs for tax or labor review:
  - payroll register
  - statutory contribution schedule
  - PIT support
  - signed approvals for unusual payouts

## 9. Reporting pack

- Prepare at minimum:
  - month-end P&L by entity and store
  - sales bridge by channel
  - COGS bridge and wastage summary
  - payroll and prime-cost summary
  - AP and AR aging
  - bank reconciliation summary
  - exception log and unresolved items
- Keep a final close memo with:
  - what changed this month
  - material estimates or assumptions
  - journals posted after management review
  - items requiring legal or tax confirmation
