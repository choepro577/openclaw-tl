# Vietnam to IFRS or Group Reporting Map

## Contents

## 1. Bridging principle

- Start from the local statutory or tax-book trial balance.
- Create a separate bridge sheet for management or IFRS adjustments.
- Keep each adjustment reversible and documented with:
  - rationale
  - source
  - amount
  - period
  - owner

## 2. Typical bridge layers

- `Layer 1: statutory base`
  - what is posted in the local books
- `Layer 2: close-quality fixes`
  - mispostings, cutoff corrections, missing accruals, classification fixes
- `Layer 3: management adjustments`
  - store or channel reallocation, one-off normalization, internal reporting reclass
- `Layer 4: IFRS or group adjustments`
  - lease accounting, impairment, deferred tax, consolidation or intercompany logic if relevant

## 3. Common F&B adjustments

### Revenue presentation

- Gross versus net presentation for delivery or marketplace arrangements may differ by principal-agent assessment.
- Gift cards, vouchers, loyalty points, and advance collections may require deferred-revenue treatment rather than immediate revenue.
- Refunds, breakage, and promotional funding from vendors or platforms should be analyzed separately from base sales.

### Lease and occupancy

- Local books may expense rent directly.
- Group or IFRS reporting may require lease liability and right-of-use accounting, plus separation of variable lease components.

### Inventory and impairment

- Local books may carry inventory until a write-off is recorded.
- Group reporting may require earlier provisioning for obsolete, slow-moving, expired, or damaged stock.
- F&B entities often need sharper provisioning for short shelf-life goods than generic trading entities.

### Payroll and employee obligations

- Bonuses, leave accruals, service charge pools, and termination obligations may require accruals earlier than local filing practices suggest.

### Provisions and contingencies

- Litigation, tax exposures, onerous contracts, or store-closure costs may need separate management or group-level review.

### Intercompany and branch logic

- Central-kitchen transfers, franchise support charges, management fees, and shared-service allocations often need elimination or reclassification in group reporting.

## 4. Suggested working-paper structure

- Tab 1: local trial balance
- Tab 2: close-quality fixes
- Tab 3: management adjustments
- Tab 4: IFRS or group adjustments
- Tab 5: mapped financial statements
- Tab 6: support links and evidence index

For each bridge entry, document:

| Field           | Expectation                                   |
| --------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Adjustment ID   | Stable identifier such as `ADJ-2026-03-01`    |
| Basis           | Statutory, management, or IFRS                |
| Account impact  | Debit and credit accounts or narrative effect |
| P&L / BS impact | Clear presentation impact                     |
| Period          | Accounting month and effective date           |
| Evidence        | Source file, memo, contract, or calculation   |
| Reviewer note   | Why this is accepted or still pending         |

Never hide a local-book issue inside a management bridge. Fix base-book errors first, then bridge to the reporting view.
